A CLEARER WAY

A dollar token, with clear rules.

01

Exchange from your wallet

Buy and sell through the OUSD/USDC pool. SOL and USDT are available when a verified route exists. Pool prices can differ from one USDC.

02

Redeem directly

After the issuer launches, burn your OUSD and receive the same amount of USDC in one transaction. You authorize the burn. Solana network fees apply.

03

Keep control visible

Issuance, account freezing, metadata changes and program upgrades require three of five issuer approvals. Upgrades can change the issuer rules.

Before you exchange

OUSD is not a bank deposit, insured cash or a claim to direct USD redemption. The reserve asset is USDC. USDC issuer actions, depegging, contract upgrades, frozen accounts, liquidity and network failures can affect access or value. Oath may freeze individual OUSD token accounts under its published policy. A frozen account cannot transfer or redeem until thawed.

Account freezing is restricted to three-of-five issuer approval for applicable legal orders, sanctions requirements or documented security incidents. It affects OUSD in the selected token account, not unrelated wallet assets.
