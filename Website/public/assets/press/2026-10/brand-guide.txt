OATH WALLET — BRAND USAGE GUIDE
Edition 2026.10.31
Official source: https://oathwallet.org/press

PERMITTED USES

Editorial coverage
Use the supplied assets in articles, reviews, interviews, educational material and factual reporting about Oath Wallet. Critical or independent coverage does not need to imply endorsement.

Accurate product listings
Use the app icon or supplied logo to identify Oath in wallet directories, supported-wallet selectors and compatibility listings when the stated support is accurate.

Faithful reproduction
Resize proportionally, select an approved light or dark variant, and place it on a simple background with sufficient contrast and clear space.

USES THAT ARE NOT PERMITTED

Changing the identity
Do not stretch, rotate, redraw, recolour, rearrange or separate the rings. Do not add gradients, outlines, shadows, effects or decorative containers to the supplied artwork.

Implying a relationship
Do not imply sponsorship, certification, investment, employment, endorsement or partnership without written approval. A wallet listing or working connection alone is not a partnership.

Impersonation or confusing branding
Do not make Oath’s marks your own app icon, account identity, domain branding, token logo or company identity. Do not use them in phishing, deceptive promotions or counterfeit products.

Unapproved commercial branding
Ask before using Oath’s marks in co-branded campaigns, paid promotional creatives, merchandise, packaging or a modified lockup. Editorial coverage and accurate compatibility listings remain permitted as described above.

Misleading product claims
Do not claim guaranteed returns, guaranteed safety, insurance, regulatory approval, independent audits or a partnership without current supporting evidence. Do not present illustrative balances as customer funds or investment performance.

ARTWORK GUIDELINES

Clear space
Keep at least one quarter of the visible symbol’s height clear on every side. For the horizontal logo, measure from the symbol. This space is outside the visible artwork; do not count transparent file padding twice.

Minimum size
Recommended screen widths: app icon 32 px, standalone symbol 24 px, horizontal logo 120 px. For print, start at 8 mm, 6 mm and 30 mm respectively. Always check legibility in the final output.

Background & contrast
Use the ink mark or wordmark on a light background and the white versions on a dark background. The blue app icon works on either. Avoid busy photographs and backgrounds that hide the mark.

File choice
Use SVG for scalable app icons and symbols. Use transparent PNG for raster layouts and the supplied horizontal wordmarks. Use the complete icon file without changing its rounded silhouette.

Name & writing
Use “Oath Wallet” on first mention and “Oath” thereafter. Keep this spelling and capitalisation. For historical context only, the app was previously called Aperture; use Oath Wallet in new headlines and listings.

Product imagery
Use the supplied product previews without changing interface labels, balances or controls. You may scale them proportionally and crop surrounding whitespace. Do not crop away context in a way that changes the meaning. Previews are illustrative and may differ from the current release.
