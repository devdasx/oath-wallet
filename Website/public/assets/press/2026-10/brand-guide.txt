OATH WALLET — BRAND NOTES
Updated October 30, 2026

Name: Oath Wallet. Short name: Oath.
Current app icon: Solid Signal Blue with white interlocking rings.
Colours: Signal Blue #247BFF; White #FFFFFF; Ink #0B0B0D.
Use a flat #247BFF background. Do not add gradients.

Use the provided SVG masters when a scalable symbol is required.
Use the PNG artwork for layouts that need a raster image.
The square app-icon source is 1024 x 1024. Its rounded corners on the
press page are a display treatment; the source file remains square.

Preserve the supplied shapes and proportions. Leave clear space around
the mark. Use the white symbol on blue or dark backgrounds and the ink
symbol on white or light backgrounds. Do not stretch, redraw, recolour,
or add shadows to the logo. Use the current blue identity instead of the
former multicolour app icon for new coverage.

The horizontal logo artwork uses the original Oath wordmark. Do not
retype or reconstruct the wordmark as a substitute for these files.

Product previews use illustrative balances and activity. They are
editorial product images, not live balances or financial projections.
Features and availability vary by network and app version.
