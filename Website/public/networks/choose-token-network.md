# Know the route before you send.

Prepare a token transfer with Oath. Start with USDT or USDC, or open any catalog token’s checklist to compare its exact identity, receiving network and fee requirements.

[Get Oath for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6780187283)

- Start with the sender. Open its available withdrawal networks for the exact token. If your intended network is not offered, do not use another network’s address as a substitute.
- Match the receiving account. In Oath, open Receive on the same network. Compare the exact contract or mint on the token page below and with the issuer’s current information.
- Plan for a later send. Check the native fee asset and any account requirements. Fees alone do not determine compatibility.
- Check before confirming. Compare the full receiving address, network and token at the sender. A small first transfer can help verify the route.

These entries come from Oath’s released catalog. Bridged and wrapped tokens may be listed separately. No selection here moves funds, bridges tokens, connects a wallet or establishes what an exchange supports.

Before you send

## Prepare your transfer.

Choose the token and the network offered by the sender. Compare its exact identity, get your receiving address in Oath, and plan for network fees.

Start with USDT or USDC here, or [find another token](https://oathwallet.org/tokens) and use its “Prepare a transfer” link. A matching symbol or address alone does not establish compatibility.

## If the network or token does not match

Stop before sending. An identical EVM address does not join balances across networks. Importing the same keys does not bridge tokens. A matching symbol does not establish that two contracts are the same asset.

If a transfer has already been broadcast, keep its public transaction ID and check the exact network, destination and token in the explorer. A completed exchange withdrawal request is not necessarily a confirmed on-chain transfer. Contact the receiving service about its recovery scope when needed.
