# Celo wallet: send and receive CELO

Receive, send and manage assets on Celo with Oath for iPhone and iPad. Keep your network, address and fee details in view.

## Receive Celo

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Celo in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Use the Celo network and review the fee asset selected by the app.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send CELO

1. Open Send and select the asset on Celo. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep CELO available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible Celo dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, Celo network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive Celo in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Celo in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Use the Celo network and review the fee asset selected by the app.

### What pays the Celo transaction fee?

CELO is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep CELO available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.

### Can I connect Celo dApps?

Connect to compatible Celo dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
