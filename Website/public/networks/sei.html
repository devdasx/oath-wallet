Sei · SEI

Sei, in your wallet.

Receive, send and manage assets on Sei with Oath for iPhone and iPad. Keep your network, address and fee details in view.

01 / Receive

Receive Sei

Your address. The right network. This account uses Sei EVM. Confirm the receiving service accepts the EVM address and network.

  1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Sei in Receive.
  2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
  3. This account uses Sei EVM. Confirm the receiving service accepts the EVM address and network.
  4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.
02 / Send

Send SEI with clarity.

Keep SEI available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.

  1. Open Send and select the asset on Sei. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
  2. Keep SEI available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.
  3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
  4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.
03 / Connect

Your wallet, connected.

Connect to compatible Sei dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

  1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
  2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
  3. Review the website, selected wallet, Sei network and requested permissions before approving.
  4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.
A few useful answers

Before you transfer.

How do I receive Sei in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Sei in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. This account uses Sei EVM. Confirm the receiving service accepts the EVM address and network.

What pays the Sei transaction fee?

SEI is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep SEI available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.

Can I connect Sei dApps?

Connect to compatible Sei dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Keep recovery details private.

Oath’s website never needs your recovery phrase or private key. Share only the receiving address when someone needs to send you crypto.
