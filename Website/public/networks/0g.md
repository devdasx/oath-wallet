# 0G wallet: send and receive 0G

Receive, send and manage assets on 0G with Oath for iPhone and iPad. Keep your network, address and fee details in view.

## Receive 0G

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose 0G in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Use the 0G mainnet EVM network, chain ID 16661, rather than a test network.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send 0G

1. Open Send and select the asset on 0G. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep 0G available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible 0G dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, 0G network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive 0G in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose 0G in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Use the 0G mainnet EVM network, chain ID 16661, rather than a test network.

### What pays the 0G transaction fee?

0G is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep 0G available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.

### Can I connect 0G dApps?

Connect to compatible 0G dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
