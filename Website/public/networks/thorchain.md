# THORChain wallet: send and receive RUNE

Keep RUNE and supported THORChain assets in Oath, and connect to compatible THORChain dApps.

## Receive THORChain

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose THORChain in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Use the THORChain address shown for your wallet. RUNE on THORChain is different from an asset represented on another network.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send RUNE

1. Open Send and select the asset on THORChain. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Review any memo, destination and fee carefully. A cross-chain swap is a separate dApp action, not an ordinary transfer to another chain.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible THORChain dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, THORChain network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive THORChain in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose THORChain in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Use the THORChain address shown for your wallet. RUNE on THORChain is different from an asset represented on another network.

### What pays the THORChain transaction fee?

RUNE is the native fee asset for this network account. Review any memo, destination and fee carefully. A cross-chain swap is a separate dApp action, not an ordinary transfer to another chain.

### Can I connect THORChain dApps?

Connect to compatible THORChain dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
