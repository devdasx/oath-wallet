# Unichain wallet: send and receive ETH

Receive, send and manage assets on Unichain with Oath for iPhone and iPad. Keep your network, address and fee details in view.

## Receive Unichain

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Unichain in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. ETH on Unichain stays on Unichain unless you use a separate bridge or swap.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send ETH

1. Open Send and select the asset on Unichain. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep ETH available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible Unichain dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, Unichain network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive Unichain in Oath?

### What pays the Unichain transaction fee?

ETH is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep ETH available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.

### Can I connect Unichain dApps?

Connect to compatible Unichain dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.
