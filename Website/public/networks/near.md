# NEAR Protocol wallet: send and receive NEAR

Receive NEAR and supported tokens, send from Oath and review compatible dApp requests.

## Receive NEAR Protocol

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose NEAR Protocol in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Share the exact account identifier shown in Receive. Confirm whether the destination expects a named NEAR account or an implicit account.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send NEAR

1. Open Send and select the asset on NEAR Protocol. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. NEAR can be needed for fees and storage. Token balances and the amount available to send may differ when storage is required.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible NEAR Protocol dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, NEAR Protocol network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive NEAR Protocol in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose NEAR Protocol in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Share the exact account identifier shown in Receive. Confirm whether the destination expects a named NEAR account or an implicit account.

### What pays the NEAR Protocol transaction fee?

NEAR is the native fee asset for this network account. NEAR can be needed for fees and storage. Token balances and the amount available to send may differ when storage is required.

### Can I connect NEAR Protocol dApps?

Connect to compatible NEAR Protocol dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.
