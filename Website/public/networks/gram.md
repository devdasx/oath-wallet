# Gram wallet: send and receive GRAM

Send and receive Gram on iPhone and iPad with Oath Wallet. Gram (GRAM) is the native coin of the TON blockchain, formerly called Toncoin.

## Receive Gram

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Gram in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Choose Gram in Receive and share the TON address shown in Oath. Confirm the sender supports the address format and add a comment if the receiving service requires one.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send GRAM

1. Open Send and select the asset on Gram. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep Gram for network fees and any wallet-contract activation. For a jetton, check its master contract; a token named GRAM is not necessarily the native coin.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible Gram dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app. WalletConnect and TON Connect are different protocols. This guide does not claim TON Connect support.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, Gram network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### Is Gram the same as TON?

Gram (GRAM) is the native coin formerly called Toncoin. The blockchain remains The Open Network (TON). Confirm you are selecting the native coin, not a similarly named jetton.

### How do I receive Gram in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Gram in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Choose Gram in Receive and share the TON address shown in Oath. Confirm the sender supports the address format and add a comment if the receiving service requires one.

### What pays the Gram transaction fee?

GRAM is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep Gram for network fees and any wallet-contract activation. For a jetton, check its master contract; a token named GRAM is not necessarily the native coin.

### Can I connect Gram dApps?

Connect to compatible Gram dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app. WalletConnect and TON Connect are different protocols. This guide does not claim TON Connect support.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.
