# Aptos wallet: send and receive APT

Manage APT and supported Aptos assets in a native iPhone and iPad wallet.

## Receive Aptos

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Aptos in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Receive on Aptos using your selected account. Match the asset type as well as the network when receiving a token.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send APT

1. Open Send and select the asset on Aptos. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep APT available for network fees when transferring supported assets.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Aptos send and receive support does not include a supported dApp connection route in this guide. Use Oath for transfers; do not import your recovery phrase into a website to connect.



## Questions

### How do I receive Aptos in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Aptos in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Receive on Aptos using your selected account. Match the asset type as well as the network when receiving a token.

### What pays the Aptos transaction fee?

APT is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep APT available for network fees when transferring supported assets.

### Can I connect Aptos dApps?

Aptos send and receive support does not include a supported dApp connection route in this guide. Use Oath for transfers; do not import your recovery phrase into a website to connect.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
