# XRP Ledger wallet: send and receive XRP

Receive XRP, manage supported XRP Ledger assets and review outgoing transfers in Oath.

## Receive XRP Ledger

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose XRP Ledger in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. A destination tag may be mandatory when sending to an exchange. Use the exact tag provided by the recipient.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send XRP

1. Open Send and select the asset on XRP Ledger. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. XRP Ledger accounts retain a network reserve. Issued assets can require a trustline; the total balance may exceed the spendable balance.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible XRP Ledger dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, XRP Ledger network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive XRP Ledger in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose XRP Ledger in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. A destination tag may be mandatory when sending to an exchange. Use the exact tag provided by the recipient.

### What pays the XRP Ledger transaction fee?

XRP is the native fee asset for this network account. XRP Ledger accounts retain a network reserve. Issued assets can require a trustline; the total balance may exceed the spendable balance.

### Can I connect XRP Ledger dApps?

Connect to compatible XRP Ledger dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.
