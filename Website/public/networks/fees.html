Find your network’s fee asset.

Find the fee asset and account requirements for Oath’s supported networks. Understand TRX, ETH, SOL and Bitcoin fees before sending.

Get Oath for iPhone

Check the fee asset for the exact network. USDT and USDC token balances generally do not replace it; a network that uses USDC as its native fee asset is identified below. ETH on Ethereum cannot pay a Base transaction, even if the account address is identical.

This is a fee-asset reference, not a live price quote. Network conditions, transaction size, contract execution and account creation can change the final requirement. Read the estimate in Oath before confirming.

Before adding a fee balance

TRON: TRX and resources

TRC-20 transfers use Energy and Bandwidth; insufficient resources can require TRX. Recipient activation can add requirements. The fee for a simple TRX transfer is not a reliable estimate for sending USDT.

Base and Ethereum: separate ETH balances

Keep ETH on the network where you intend to send. Base’s fee can include both execution and data-publication costs. Do not assume Oath offers a sponsored fee just because another dApp does.

Solana: SOL and token accounts

Keep SOL for transaction fees and any required token-account creation. A displayed token quantity does not establish that the fee requirements are covered.

Bitcoin: BTC and transaction size

The fee depends on the transaction’s size and selected inputs. Use Oath’s fee review and Max calculation; there is no fixed fee that applies to every send.
