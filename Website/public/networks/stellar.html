01 / Receive

Receive Stellar

Your address. The right network. For an exchange deposit, include the memo exactly as required by the recipient. An address alone may not identify your exchange account.

  1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Stellar in Receive.
  2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
  3. For an exchange deposit, include the memo exactly as required by the recipient. An address alone may not identify your exchange account.
  4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.
02 / Send

Send XLM with clarity.

Stellar accounts have reserve requirements. Issued assets can require a trustline; check the recipient and the available balance.

  1. Open Send and select the asset on Stellar. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
  2. Stellar accounts have reserve requirements. Issued assets can require a trustline; check the recipient and the available balance.
  3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
  4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.
03 / Connect

Your wallet, connected.

Connect to compatible Stellar dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

  1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
  2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
  3. Review the website, selected wallet, Stellar network and requested permissions before approving.
  4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.
A few useful answers

Before you transfer.

How do I receive Stellar in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Stellar in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. For an exchange deposit, include the memo exactly as required by the recipient. An address alone may not identify your exchange account.

What pays the Stellar transaction fee?

XLM is the native fee asset for this network account. Stellar accounts have reserve requirements. Issued assets can require a trustline; check the recipient and the available balance.

Can I connect Stellar dApps?

Connect to compatible Stellar dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Keep recovery details private.

Oath’s website never needs your recovery phrase or private key. Share only the receiving address when someone needs to send you crypto.
