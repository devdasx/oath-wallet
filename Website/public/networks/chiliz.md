# Chiliz wallet: send and receive CHZ

Receive, send and manage assets on Chiliz with Oath for iPhone and iPad. Keep your network, address and fee details in view.

## Receive Chiliz

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Chiliz in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Use Chiliz Chain rather than an Ethereum deployment of a token with the same symbol.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send CHZ

1. Open Send and select the asset on Chiliz. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep CHZ available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible Chiliz dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, Chiliz network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive Chiliz in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Chiliz in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Use Chiliz Chain rather than an Ethereum deployment of a token with the same symbol.

### What pays the Chiliz transaction fee?

CHZ is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep CHZ available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.

### Can I connect Chiliz dApps?

Connect to compatible Chiliz dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.
