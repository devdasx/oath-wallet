# TRON wallet: send and receive TRX

Manage TRX and supported TRC-20 assets with clear send and receive details.

## Receive TRON

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose TRON in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Choose TRON and confirm the token contract. A TRC-20 deposit must use TRON, even when the same token also exists elsewhere.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send TRX

1. Open Send and select the asset on TRON. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. TRON uses bandwidth and energy. A token transfer may consume TRX when resources are insufficient; review the estimate first.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect to compatible TRON dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, TRON network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive TRON in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose TRON in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Choose TRON and confirm the token contract. A TRC-20 deposit must use TRON, even when the same token also exists elsewhere.

### What pays the TRON transaction fee?

TRX is the native fee asset for this network account. TRON uses bandwidth and energy. A token transfer may consume TRX when resources are insufficient; review the estimate first.

### Can I connect TRON dApps?

Connect to compatible TRON dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
