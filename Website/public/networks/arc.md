# Arc wallet: send and receive USDC

Receive, send and manage assets on Arc with Oath for iPhone and iPad. Keep your network, address and fee details in view.

## Receive Arc

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Arc in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Arc uses USDC for native transaction fees. Its separate transfer path does not currently expose WalletConnect transaction signing.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send USDC

1. Open Send and select the asset on Arc. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep USDC available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Arc send and receive support does not include a supported dApp connection route in this guide. Use Oath for transfers; do not import your recovery phrase into a website to connect.



## Questions

### How do I receive Arc in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Arc in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Arc uses USDC for native transaction fees. Its separate transfer path does not currently expose WalletConnect transaction signing.

### What pays the Arc transaction fee?

USDC is the native fee asset for this network account. Keep USDC available for network fees. For tokens, check the contract and amount, then review the destination and estimated fee in Oath.

### Can I connect Arc dApps?

Arc send and receive support does not include a supported dApp connection route in this guide. Use Oath for transfers; do not import your recovery phrase into a website to connect.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
