Oath — A wallet, not a marketplace.

Oath is a self-custody Bitcoin and crypto wallet for iPhone and iPad. You control your wallet credentials, review fees before confirming, and manage supported assets without a trading marketplace.

  • Download Oath on the App Store — free, for iPhone and iPad
  • Features · Support · Press kit

Why Oath

Most crypto apps want you to trade. Oath wants you to own. No tickers shouting, no casino lights, no one holding your coins for you. Just your money, kept.

What Oath supports

  • Keep wallet credentials on your device and sign transactions locally.
  • Create a recovery phrase, or use physical entropy with care.
  • Restore supported wallet formats; keep any extra BIP-39 passphrase.
  • Use optional password-encrypted iCloud backup with its matching backup file.
  • Choose supported Bitcoin address types and inspect EVM approvals.
  • Review network fees before confirming a transaction.
  • Read the public source and security scope.

Capabilities depend on the network and release. Silent Payments receiving and discovery are not supported in 4.1.14.

Self-custody, explained

What is a self-custody crypto wallet?

A self-custody, or non-custodial, wallet lets you authorize transactions with your wallet’s private keys. The crypto remains recorded on its blockchain. Your wallet helps you manage the credentials and addresses used to access it. Keeping a usable recovery method is your responsibility.

Understand Oath’s security model

How is a wallet different from an exchange account?

With a custodial exchange account, the provider controls the withdrawal keys. With self-custody, you manage the signing credentials. This removes reliance on an exchange to approve a withdrawal, but it also means a forgotten recovery phrase, a malicious approval or a mistaken transfer can cause permanent loss.

Explore Oath Wallet features

Can I use Oath for Bitcoin and other crypto on iPhone?

Oath Wallet is available for iPhone and iPad. Choose the asset and network in the app before sharing a receiving address. A matching coin name or address format does not mean two networks are interchangeable. Available actions depend on your app version, network and wallet type.

Check supported networks

What happens if I lose my phone?

Recovery depends on the backup you prepared before the loss. A recovery phrase may also require its separate BIP-39 passphrase. Oath’s optional iCloud recovery needs the encrypted backup and the backup password. Check your recovery method before relying on it, and never share recovery words with support.

Read the backup checklist

Is Oath free, and why do transfers have fees?

Oath is free to download from the App Store. Blockchain transfers can require network fees, account reserves or activation costs. These vary by network and conditions. Review the asset, destination, network and fee before confirming; an on-chain transfer generally cannot be reversed.

Learn about network fees

Does open source mean a wallet has been audited?

Public code lets people inspect an implementation. It does not by itself establish an independent security audit or prove that the app installed on a phone matches that code. Oath publishes its source-review scope and limitations so you can check the evidence behind a claim.
