# About Oath Wallet — Team, Open Source & Official Links

Oath Wallet is a free, open-source Bitcoin and crypto wallet for iPhone and iPad.



## What Oath does

Oath is a self-custody wallet: you control the recovery credentials and authorize transactions on your device. It brings supported wallet imports, Bitcoin address choices, network-fee review, WalletConnect and optional encrypted iCloud backup into one app. Supported features depend on the network, wallet type and release.

[Explore the features](/features), [check the supported networks](/networks), or start with the [backup and recovery guide](/articles/backup-restore-oath-wallet).

## Product guides and corrections

Oath Editorial is the team byline for Oath’s product documentation, not an independent product review or security certification.

### What a reviewed guide means

Capability claims are checked against the named release. Compatibility guides explain the account formats checked, the evidence and its limits. Source review, host tests and completed live transactions are distinct evidence. Original app captures retain their version and sample-data labels. A catalog listing is discovery evidence, not issuer verification or a safety endorsement.

### Updates and corrections

Modification dates reflect content changes, not deployments. Historical screenshots and records keep their original scope. Read the [release history](https://articles.oathwallet.org/oath-wallet-release-history).

For a correction, email [care@oathwallet.org](mailto:care@oathwallet.org) with the page URL and issue, never recovery credentials. Follow the [security policy](https://github.com/OathWallet/oath-wallet/security/policy) for vulnerabilities.

## Help and vulnerability reports

For product guidance, use the [support page](/support) and [practical guides](/articles). For a security issue, follow the [repository’s reporting policy](https://github.com/OathWallet/oath-wallet/security/policy). That policy describes the status of its private reporting channel; do not post exploit details in a public issue while arranging a private channel.

Never send a recovery phrase, private key, passphrase, backup credential or passkey to anyone claiming to be support. Oath cannot recover missing credentials or reverse an on-chain transaction.
