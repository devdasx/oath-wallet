# Oath Wallet 4.1.15 (99)

## Changes

- WalletConnect review and signing for Bitcoin, TON, TRON, Polkadot, Sui, NEAR, Stellar and XRP Ledger. Supported methods vary by chain; requests remain bound to the approved account, network and payload.
- Bitcoin PSBT, message signing and transfer review; TON transaction/data signing and proof requests; NEAR scoped access-key permissions; chain-specific transaction details and preflight checks.
- Local durable submission journals, pending-send reservations and restart recovery for supported transaction flows, avoiding overlapping account state and unnecessary re-signing.
- Network-aware payment scanner review, larger recipient review sheets, and refreshed Chiliz, Filecoin, IoTeX, Polkadot, Sei and 0G artwork.
- Pinned Stellar SDK and bundled XRP transaction codec, with their license notices and deterministic fixture generators.

## Privacy

Recovery input and private keys remain on-device. Diagnostics remain local. Optional encrypted iCloud backup, WalletConnect relay communication, blockchain queries and signed transaction broadcasts retain their existing purposes.
