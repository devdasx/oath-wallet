# Oath Wallet 4.1.14 (98)

## Changes

- WalletConnect access reviews show the wallet, token, spender and editable allowance more clearly. THORChain swap reviews show source/destination assets, minimum payout, fees and timing without changing the signing request.
- Connection requests and results share a stable presentation owner, preserve underlying screens, and revalidate queued requests. Network consent retains exact requested identifiers.
- Scan payment requests directly from an asset. Exact requests can advance to amount entry; ambiguous networks/assets retain selection, and missing XRP/Stellar routing information stays editable.
- Expanded Bitcoin payment alternatives, Stellar memo formats, TON links, bare-address queries and Arc native-USDC decimal handling. Memo, destination-tag and on-chain-message details remain visible in Send.
- Receive has updated artwork, address placement and network search. Insufficient native fees offer the correct sending-account deposit address and bounded quote refresh; signing still requires authorization.
- Show verified EVM block inclusion while retaining durable finality tracking. Reconcile exact duplicate broadcasts and delayed receipts without re-signing or spending twice.
- Improved IoTeX pinned reads, Celo/Monad history recovery, Sui checkpoint handling, Solana archive retries, NEAR SWEAT metadata, and Aptos/TON partial-inventory error handling.
- Bitcoin transaction details display decoded OP_RETURN messages, preserving binary data as hex. Switching wallets resets retained history navigation.

## Privacy

Wallet keys and recovery input remain on-device. Diagnostics are stored locally. Optional encrypted iCloud backup, WalletConnect and blockchain queries and signed broadcasts remain available.
