# Oath Wallet 5.0.0 (102)

- Bitcoin history batches address queries. Litecoin history preserves payment destinations across upgrades.
- Permission reviews group approvals by contract. Batch revocation tracks each approval separately.
- WalletConnect distinguishes invalid, expired and previously used codes, unsupported requests, and connection failures.
- Opening an existing wallet preserves accounts on unfamiliar networks and handles historical encrypted wallet formats.
- Backup confirmation checks the saved document. Recovery words and signing keys stay on the device during ordinary wallet use.
