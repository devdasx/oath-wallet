# Oath Wallet 5.0.2 (104)

## Apple distribution status

Apple approved Oath Wallet **5.0.2 (104)**. App Store Connect shows **Ready for Distribution**, confirmed by the maintainer on **5 November 2026**.

The immutable source tags and original validation records below are unchanged. Apple approval is not an independent audit or a source-to-binary equivalence result.

## New and improved

- Transaction receipts now show sender totals, keeping transferred assets and network fees in their native units. Bitcoin receipts identify wallet-owned inputs and change outputs.
- Solana history dates affected by an earlier numeric conversion are repaired offline, including cached receipts, without changing balances or transaction data.
- Asset search and selection show symbols, full names, network badges, and native/fiat balances more clearly.
- Native system typography is used across supported languages. Completed iCloud backup status remains visible.

## Reliability and security repairs

- Preserve interrupted wallet-import recovery, wallet ordering and Bitcoin address discovery journals.
- Harden encrypted backup file handling and reject stale progress callbacks; clear revealed private keys when leaving the foreground.
- Reject malformed WalletConnect encodings and inconsistent XRP amount/simulation replies.
- Preserve pending transaction journals during removal and revoke stale wallet signing and permission consent.
- Validate history restart ownership and arithmetic, keep delayed balance observations pending, and handle sliced NEAR, Sui, XDR and SCALE buffers safely.
- Reject out-of-range Stellar plan timestamps without trapping.
