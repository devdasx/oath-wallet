## United States Mint coin artwork

The entropy heads and tails images use the matching 2013 United States half
dollar obverse and reverse published by the United States Mint. Wikimedia
Commons identifies both source images as public-domain works of the United
States Department of the Treasury.

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:US_50_Cent_Obv.png
